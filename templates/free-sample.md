## 1. The Business License Request (Day 1)

**Subject:** Supplier qualification — [Your Company] x [Supplier Name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Before we discuss pricing, we complete a standard qualification file for every new supplier. Could you send:
>
> 1. Your business license (营业执照) — clear photo or scan, all four corners visible
> 2. Export license (if you export directly)
> 3. The legal name and beneficiary name for your company bank account
>
> This is the same file we ask of all partners — it takes ten minutes and saves us both weeks later.
>
> Best, [You]

*Why it works: legitimate factories send this without blinking. Silence for 48+ hours is red flag #1.*

---

## 2. The Trap Question (Testing for trading companies)

**Subject:** Quick factory question — [Product]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Two quick things:
>
> 1. Which production lines do you own for [product type] — injection, assembly, packaging? (We'll do a video walkthrough later.)
> 2. What's the *factory* address for pickup under FOB? We sometimes arrange our own forwarder.
>
> Thanks, [You]

*Why it works: factories answer line questions instantly. Trading companies dodge, or "our partner factory handles that."*

---

## 3. The Video Audit Offer (Skip the $500 inspection)

**Subject:** Instead of third-party inspection — 20-minute video walkthrough

> Hi [Name],
>
> For the first order we normally book an inspection, but let's start simpler: a 20-minute live video walkthrough on WhatsApp/WeChat of the [product] production area, this week if possible. We'll check the line, the materials staged for our order, and meet the line supervisor.
>
> If the walkthrough goes well, we skip the inspection fee on this order.
>
> Best, [You]

---

## 4. Payment Structuring (Never 100% upfront)

**Subject:** Payment terms for PO-[number]

> Hi [Name],
>
> We're aligned on price. On payment, our company policy for first orders:
>
> - 30% deposit, T/T to the company account matching the business license
> - 70% balance after pre-shipment photos/video + BL copy
>
> We know some suppliers ask 100% for small orders — we can't do that on a first collaboration, but we'll move fast: balance within 48 hours of documents.
>
> Best, [You]

---

## 5. The Silent Supplier Revival (After They Ghost You)

**Subject:** PO-[number] — confirming schedule before we reallocate

> Hi [Name],
>
> We haven't had an update since [date]. If the [product] schedule has slipped, that's fine — we just need a date to plan around.
>
> If we don't hear back by [date + 3 business days], we'll reallocate the order to the backup supplier. Nothing personal — we'd rather keep this order with you.
>
> [You]
