# T01 · First Contact Email

> Opens the conversation like a professional buyer, not a tourist. Signals: you know the product, you know the market, you're comparing quotes.

**Subject: RFQ — [Product name] — [Company name] (US/EU market)**

```
Hello [Sales name],

I'm [Your name], sourcing manager at [Company] — we sell [product category] to the [US / EU / other] market.

We're evaluating manufacturing partners for [product, with 1-line spec: material, size, key standard]. Monthly volume at maturity: [X,XXX] units.

Three questions before we go further:

1. Is this a product you manufacture in-house, or do you trade it? (Either is fine — we just price differently.)
2. What is your MOQ and lead time for a first order of [X00] units?
3. Do you hold current export certifications for our market ([FDA / CE / FCC / CPSIA])? Please attach the certificates.

If the fit looks right, I'll send our full spec sheet and request samples against it.

Best regards,
[Name]
[Title], [Company]
[Website]
```

**Why it works:** volume signal (they quote serious buyers seriously), the in-house question (forces disclosure early), certs-first (filters out resellers who can't produce paperwork).
