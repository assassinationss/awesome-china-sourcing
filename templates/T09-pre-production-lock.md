# T09 · Pre-Production Lock Email

> The last email before money moves. Freezes the spec so "same as before" can never be argued later.

**Subject: Pre-production confirmation — PO [XXXX] — reply = approval to start**

```
Hello [Sales name],

Deposit is ready to send. Before it moves, confirm these five points by reply (your reply is our joint record):

1. **Spec**: spec sheet v1.1 (attached) governs — including tolerances table, materials, and [test standard]. No substitutions without written approval.
2. **Price**: $[X.XX]/unit FOB [port], valid for this PO.
3. **Quantity & tolerance**: [10,000] units, ±2% at ship, overage billed at PO price.
4. **Timeline**: [X] days from deposit receipt to cargo-ready-date, photos of finished goods before balance payment.
5. **Changes**: any change to spec, price, or timeline requires my written OK before proceeding.

Confirm all five and I'll send the deposit today.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

**Why it exists:** every quality dispute you'll ever have starts at this email. Signed by their reply, it converts "but you never said" into "see item 4." The ±2% quantity tolerance prevents the classic overship-and-invoice trick.
