# T02 · Verification Request Email

> Asks for the documents that separate factories from facades — phrased so a legitimate supplier feels respected, not accused.

**Subject: Supplier qualification — [Company name] x [Your company]**

```
Hello [Sales name],

Thanks for the quote — it's in a competitive range, so [Company] is moving you to shortlist.

Our compliance process (same for every supplier we work with, nothing personal) needs three items:

1. Business license — a clear copy, company name visible
2. Two export references in the [US/EU] market we may contact (name + email is enough)
3. A 15-minute video call showing the production floor — we'll schedule around your working hours

Once these clear, we'll proceed to samples and discuss the pre-production spec.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

**Why it works:** "same for every supplier" removes the accusation sting; references + video call are the two things shell companies can't fake. A real factory answers within 48h. Evasion *is* your answer.
