# T03 · Sample Order Email

> Orders samples like a professional: paid, specced, and traceable — not begged for free.

**Subject: Sample order — [Product] against spec v1.0**

```
Hello [Sales name],

Verification cleared — let's move to samples.

- Qty: [2–5] units
- Spec: attached spec sheet v1.0 (samples must match it — deviations disqualify)
- Price: we pay sample cost + express freight, PayPal or Alibaba Trade Assurance
- Address: [Your full address with phone — couriers need it]

Please confirm total and send a payment link. Also share the courier tracking within [3] days of payment.

One note: we order samples on our own account with [DHL/FedEx], from your declared value of $[X] — no need to arrange shipping.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

**Pro moves in this email:**
- **You pay** — free samples get you showroom units; paid samples get you *production-line* units
- **Spec-locked** — sample can't drift from what you'll actually order
- **Your courier account** — kills the classic inflated-freight margin trick
